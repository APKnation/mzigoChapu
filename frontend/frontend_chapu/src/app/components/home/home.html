Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about mzigoChapU.

How do payments work?

All payments are held in escrow when a booking is confirmed. Funds are released to the driver automatically once you confirm safe delivery. We support M-Pesa, Tigo Pesa, Airtel Money, and bank transfers.

Are truck drivers verified?

Yes. Every truck owner must verify their vehicle registration, insurance, and driving license before they can accept bookings. Only verified drivers appear in search results.

What if my cargo is damaged?

mzigoChapU provides cargo insurance coverage up to TSh 10,000,000 per shipment. If damage occurs during transit, file a claim through the app and our team will review it within 48 hours.

How do I track my shipment?

Every assigned trip includes live GPS tracking. You'll see your truck's real-time location on the map, estimated arrival time, and receive notifications at key milestones.

Is it free to sign up?

Yes! Creating an account is completely free. Cargo owners only pay a small service fee (2%) on completed transactions. Truck owners pay nothing — list your vehicles and start earning immediately.
