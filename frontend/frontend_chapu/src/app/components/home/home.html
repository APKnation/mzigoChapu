How It Works

  1. 1

    Create Your Account

    Sign up as either a cargo owner needing to ship goods or a truck owner with capacity to transport.

  2. 2

    Post or Browse Loads

    Cargo owners post their shipment details, truck owners browse available loads that match their route.

  3. 3

    Connect & Ship

    Accept a bid, confirm the trip, and track your cargo until it reaches its destination safely.
